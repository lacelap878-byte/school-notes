## 1. Running the protocol near tests and exams (raised by the student, S10, 2026-11-03)

The student's words: with the notebook full and the final a week away, copying notes is an inefficient use of time. The plan was to work through questions and past papers. They asked for the protocol to cover how it is used near exams and semester tests.

Evidence from S10: with no pages left, the session ran as a slow question session. Nothing in v11 describes that mode, so there was no stamp for it and no rule for what replaces the parts, the Go stop line and the page.

What the proposal would settle:
- A named mode for the weeks before a test or exam ("Question session"), chosen at STATE A, as an alternative to teach and review.
- No copy-ready parts. The notes.md still gets a full file, for reading only and never for copying.
- A state stamp for it, for example `[CDG | S[N] | [topic] | questions]`, used in this session by hand.
- The quizzes stay (start of session and end of session), the screenshots rule stays, and written drills are preferred to multiple choice when the slips show in written work.
- A first-contact rule: when a topic is new to the student, teach from one concrete example before giving the whole lecture, and ask first whether the student has met it.
- When a wording keeps bringing back a misreading (for example "forced" for degrees of freedom), record the wording that worked in the friction log and avoid the bad one.
- What happens to Memo, Revise and the final summary in this mode.

## 2. Tables as the default for an explanation that is not landing (S10)

Evidence: the student asked for a table three times when paragraphs were not working. Proposal: when a first explanation does not land, the second attempt is a small table with concrete numbers, one idea per table, before any further prose.
