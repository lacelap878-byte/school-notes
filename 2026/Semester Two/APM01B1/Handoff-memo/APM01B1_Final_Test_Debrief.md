# APM01B1 Final Test Debrief

Written 27 Oct 2026, the day after the final. This records what was in the paper in the student's own words, so it can be used if the module is reviewed or repeated, or to compare against the mark when it comes out.

## What the paper looked like

In the student's words:
- "It was easy."
- There were no line integrals.
- There was only one F = ma question, and it was very similar to the first question of ST1.
- The last question was simple harmonic motion, where A and B had to be calculated, plus some other things to prove.
- "I was really happy with my test and how it went."

## How this compares with the preparation

- Line integrals got a lot of preparation time (S3 notes, method card section 1) but did not appear. Worth knowing for a future sitting, but do not drop them from revision on the strength of one paper.
- The F = ma question matched ST1 Q1, so the path equation method in the S1 notes (integrate twice, fix the constants with r(0) and v(0)) is what it called for. See notes/APM01B1_Notes_S1_PolarIntrinsicCoordinates.md, "Two edge cases for solving m r double dot = F".
- The SHM question used the ansatz x(t) = A cos(wt) + B sin(wt), with A and B found from the initial conditions. See notes/APM01B1_Notes_S2_Dynamics.md, "Position-dependent force: springs and simple harmonic motion", and the SHM ansatz proof in Proofs/APM01B1_Proofs_ST2.md.
