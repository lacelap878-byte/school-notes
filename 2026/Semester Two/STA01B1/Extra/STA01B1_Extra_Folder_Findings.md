## What it changes

- Chi-square is very likely examinable (the Mock Exam has two questions on it), so the S10 work was well placed. The tests to expect: goodness of fit with a percentage claim, and a test of independence or homogeneity with a table.
- Lecture 6 is a real gap with exam material: the one-sample t test, the one-proportion z test and P-values. The 2021 deck says t when n < 30, which the lecturer's ruling (n > 40 throughout) replaces. Proposed as late material (follows S5) if the lecturer confirms scope.
- The Mock Exam is a ready-made timed practice paper, closer to the final's shape than 2022 Paper C and 2023 Paper A. It has no memo; answers would be worked and checked here.
- Question 1 of the Mock Exam asks for explanations in the student's own words (the idea of a 95% confidence interval, the principle of hypothesis testing in three to five sentences, the principle of ANOVA in four sentences, and interpreting Type I and Type II errors for a one-sided test). That is the plain-language skill the lecturer stresses.
